Do not commit the source PDF. The reconstructed design and its drawings retain
the attribution and CC BY-SA 3.0 licence; application functionality remains
under its source-file licences. No fictional contact or site-survey data from
the title blocks is copied into the model.

## Source ledger

| Sheets | Model evidence |
|---|---|
| A1.1 | 32 by 16 ft framing footprint; perimeter foundation and porch piers |
| A1.2–A1.3 | Two dwelling plans; 12/8/12 ft longitudinal bays; central stair and service rooms |
| A1.4 | Main gable roof, front hipped porch roof, rear shed roof; overhangs |
| A2.0–A2.3 | Four elevations and window locations |
| A3.0–A3.2 | Ground datum 0, upper floor 10 ft, ceiling 19 ft, ridge 29 ft 2 in |
| A1.7, A3.2 | 16 equal risers, quarter-turn winders at both ends |
| A4.0, A4.3 | Wall build-ups; windows 30 by 60 and 24 by 48 in; door sizes |

All source feet/inches are converted with exactly 0.3048 m/ft and 0.0254 m/in.
The source has conflicting roof slope labels (12/12 and 13/12). The adaptation
uses written eave/ridge datums and the roof-plan overhang to determine the
actual model pitch, rather than silently claiming all three agree. Foundation
depths, unlabelled finish thicknesses and simplified fixture geometry are
explicit reconstruction assumptions. This is an educational reference design,
not an independently engineered construction document.
